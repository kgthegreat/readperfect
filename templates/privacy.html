Privacy

Private by default.

readperfect is designed for private, invitation-based review. We collect only the account, book, invitation, and feedback data needed to operate the workspace.

Author accounts include name, email address, and password hash. Invited reviewers provide an email address and any feedback they choose to submit.

Book feedback is visible only within the workspace for that book and is not published publicly by default.

We use Google OAuth only when enabled by the site operator. Otherwise the service runs on email and password authentication only.

If you are participating in a private review and want your data removed, contact the site operator through the address listed on the contact page.
