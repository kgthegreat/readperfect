Built for private early feedback

Authors start the workspace. Reviewers join by invitation.

readperfect is designed for deliberate early review: authors add a book, invite trusted readers, and receive feedback that can be drafted over time and submitted with context.

For authors

Invite trusted readers into a private feedback workspace.

  • Add a book by title and author, then enrich it with metadata.
  • Invite family, friends, or early readers by email.
  • Ask specific questions alongside open-ended page notes.
  • Read submitted feedback by reviewer, page, or chapter.

For invited reviewers

Respond in detail without being pushed into rushed reactions.

  • Join by invitation, then sign in with Google or email.
  • Leave page-by-page commentary or broader chapter reflections.
  • Save drafts as you read, then send feedback deliberately.
  • Share feedback directly with the author in a private workspace.

How it works

A clear flow from first invite to finished feedback.

01

Create an account

Authors can sign in with Google or create an email and password account, then land in a dedicated workspace.

02

Add a book

Start with a title and author, then enrich the book with metadata and custom author questions.

03

Invite reviewers

Authors invite family, friends, or early readers by email and guide them into one private feedback workspace.

04

Read submitted feedback

Reviewers draft freely, then submit intentionally. Authors read feedback in one calm workspace.

Privacy and trust

Early feedback should stay controlled.

Private by default

Reviewer notes stay private between the invited reviewer and the author unless the author opens visibility later.

Invitation-based participation

In V1, books are created by authors and feedback is collected only from reviewers the author chooses to invite.

Deliberate submission

Feedback can be drafted over time, then sent when a reviewer is ready to stand behind it.
